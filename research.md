# Research
Motivated by my own experience as a disabled engineering student, my research focuses on disability in higher engineering education. I primarily collect qualitative data through interviews. My publications include reviews of existing disability literature, experiences of ableism, and best practices for higher ed instructors to create a classroom inclusive to disabled students. 

To view my published works, navigate to my Publications tab or you can visit my <a href=" https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Emily-Landgren?ev=hdr_xprf">ResearchGate</a> or <a href=" https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=N4YZVCgAAAAJ&hl=en&authuser=1">Google Scholar</a>. <br>
